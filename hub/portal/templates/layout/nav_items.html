{# The navigation, as data — the single source shared by `layout/nav.html` (sidebar + bottom tab bar) and `layout/topbar.html` (page icon and title). The heading is drawn by `layout/nav.html` when the group CHANGES between two rendered entries, so a group whose every entry is gated away for this role leaves no orphan heading behind. Order follows what you WATCH, then the DEVICES, then what you ADMINISTER. The watch block runs live-to-retrospective (Monitoring, Alarms, History); the devices block goes top-down through the hardware (Hubs, Nodes, Beacons); the admin block starts with people and ends at the log. The Hubs entry is labelled Hubs rather than Device because Nodes and Beacons are devices too, and the product calls this one the Hub. It points at the list (`/hubs`); a single Hub is `/hubs/{hub}/overview` (plan/portal/devices.hub.md §3). `settings` is ONE door into an area of two sections — Notifications and Maintenance — which are its tabs (partials/settings_tabs.html), not nav entries of their own. `/settings` is a redirect to whichever the visitor's role opens first, so the entry never leads nowhere. Users is NOT one of them, deliberately.
